## TL;DR
> Sabrina Ramonov shares five short "secret codes" you can type inline in any prompt to change how ChatGPT (and Claude, or any AI) responds: "truth mode" for unfiltered pushback, "/human" to de-slop AI writing, "eli10" for jargon-free explanations, "future you" for advice voiced by your already-successful future self, and "red team" to make the model attack your plan like an adversary. No settings or custom instructions required — you just type the keyword alongside a normal prompt.

**Watch this if:** you want lightweight prompt modifiers that flip ChatGPT out of agreeable-generic mode without permanently changing your custom instructions.

## Key Takeaways
- "Truth mode" makes [[topics/ChatGPT]] stop glazing and agreeing — it surfaces the weakest parts of your thinking and what you're overlooking.
- Inline codes beat custom preferences: a preference like "always push back" applies to every chat (even email cleanups), while a typed code applies only when you want it.
- "/human" humanizes output for social posts and writing, replacing a very long humanizer prompt that would otherwise eat your whole customization settings — still review and add your own voice.
- "eli10" (explain like I'm 10) is a fast way to learn new topics like ad metrics without wading through technical jargon.
- "future you" has ChatGPT use everything it knows about you to answer as your future self who already achieved the goal — a fun way to see what the model actually believes you'll do.
- "red team", borrowed from cybersecurity, turns the model into a sparring partner that rips apart your business plan: weak arguments, blind spots, riskiest assumptions, missing data. Works in any AI, including [[topics/Claude]].
