# Introducing Claude Dashboards and Claude Motion

## TL;DR
> Anthropic announces two new artifact types in beta: Claude Dashboards (connects to your data platform or CRM and builds dashboards that update as the data changes — beta on all paid plans) and Claude Motion (turns a report or chart into a short animated explainer written entirely as code, no video model involved, so every word, number, and timing is editable, exportable as MP4 or into your video editor — beta on Team and Enterprise). The demo walks a bike-share operations story from warehouse data to live dashboard to shareable, commentable animated narrative. Claude Docs, Claude Slides, and Claude Design simultaneously exit beta and go free for everyone.

**Watch this if:** you want a 90-second look at how Claude now turns live business data into dashboards and those dashboards into editable animated explainers for all-hands or board decks.

## Key Takeaways
- [[topics/Claude Dashboards]] connects to a data platform or CRM and builds dashboards that stay live as the underlying data changes; in beta on all paid plans.
- [[topics/Claude Motion]] turns a report, chart, or dashboard into a short animated explainer — [[topics/Claude]] writes it as code with no video model involved, so any word, number, or timing can be changed, then exported as an MP4 or opened in a video editor; beta on Team and Enterprise plans.
- The demo workflow: bike-share docks empty by 8:15 every weekday → Claude pulls a year of ride data from the warehouse → dashboard reveals the story → one ask converts it into an animated narrative for the all-hands.
- Iteration happens via comments directly on the video, and colleagues can comment too — the story is built collaboratively, with export into design tools when the team wants to go further.
- Also announced: [[topics/Claude Docs]], [[topics/Claude Slides]], and [[topics/Claude Design]] are out of beta and available on every plan, including Free.
