## TL;DR
> A rundown of Anthropic's newly opened Fellows Program: a remote-friendly, 4-month, 40-hour/week paid position ($3,850/week, roughly $15k/month) for AI safety, AI security, ML systems, reinforcement learning, and economics fellows, with an unusually short application.

**Watch this if:** you have a technical background (CS, math, physics, economics, or cybersecurity) and want a paid, full-time-track entry point into AI safety research at Anthropic.

## Key Takeaways
- Anthropic's Fellows Program pays a base of $3,850/week (roughly $15,000/month) for a 40-hour week, running 4 months with a possible extension.
- Anthropic explicitly encourages applicants who don't meet every listed qualification to apply anyway, citing imposter syndrome research among underrepresented groups.
- Open work streams include AI safety, AI security, ML systems and performance, reinforcement learning, and economics fellows.
- The program description says candidates "must be fluent in Python," but the actual per-cohort minimum requirements listed further down are less strict — at minimum, some form of bachelor's degree.
- Strong candidates are motivated by AI safety/societal benefit, want to transition into empirical AI research (potentially leading to a full-time Anthropic role), and have a relevant technical or social-science background.
- The application itself is short — resume, degree/coursework info — and can reportedly be completed in a few minutes.
- The presenter suggests pasting the program's requirements into ChatGPT or Claude if the jargon is unclear, since the language can be dense.
